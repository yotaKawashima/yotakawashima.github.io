About

My research interest is in understanding how humans perceive the world and building computational models that make this understanding actionable. I'm currently at the Bernstein Center for Computational Neuroscience Berlin, and part of the Explainable AI (XAI) group at Fraunhofer HHI. Before that, I spent several years researching consciousness and perception in Japan and Australia.
